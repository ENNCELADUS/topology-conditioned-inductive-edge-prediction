03 / TRAIN

Training objective

edge-active, reconstruction-aware
\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal L &= \mathcal L_{\mathrm{edge}} + 0.5\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{real}} + 0.1\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ssl}} + \mathcal L_{\mathrm{recon}}, \\ \mathcal L_{\mathrm{recon}} &= \mathcal L_{\mathrm{feat}} + 0.5\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{exist}} + 0.25\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{mult}} + 0.5\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{deg}} \\ &\quad + 0.5\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{slotadj}} + 0.25\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{gate}} + 0.25\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ptr}} + 0.5\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{align}} + 0.1\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{div}} + 0.25\,\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rel}}. \end{aligned} \]

\(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{edge}}\) is the binary queried-pair objective. \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{real}}\) matches generated and real train-side ego statistics and random-GIN embeddings; \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{ssl}}\) enforces feature-noise and pool-resampling consistency. Only \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{feat}}\), \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{exist}}\), \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{mult}}\), and \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{deg}}\) anneal from \(1\) to \(0.25\) during the edge-active phase; the relational repair terms remain fully weighted.

Qualification sequence

\(V_{\mathrm{fit}}\) calibration → freeze thresholds and implementation → one prospective \(V_{\mathrm{qual}}\) rehearsal, with at most three disclosed attempts. Any failed G3 gate blocks binding. \(V_{\mathrm{select}}\) remains sealed until the first bound formal run.

Collapse abort

After conditioning activates, abort as training_invalid(slot_collapse) if mean pairwise slot-\(h\) cosine is \(>0.95\), or if \(\lVert\Pi-r c^\top/m\rVert_F/\lVert\Pi\rVert_F<0.05\), for two consecutive validations.
