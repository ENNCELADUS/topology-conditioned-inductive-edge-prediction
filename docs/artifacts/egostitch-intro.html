Topology-conditioned inductive edge prediction · rev-3.2

EgoStitch

Imagine two endpoint neighborhoods, align their latent slots, and stitch them into generated local topology that conditions a symmetric binary edge classifier.

01 / FLOW

From endpoint features to an edge probability

Model data flow

Inputs and output. For a queried endpoint pair \((u,v)\), the model receives raw endpoint token streams \(E_u,E_v\), frozen pooled endpoint features \(x_u,x_v\), and endpoint-specific grounding candidates \(G_u,G_v\). Its only task output is the probability \(p_{uv}\) that the queried edge exists; generated topology remains intermediate conditioning context.

\[ \begin{aligned} &E_r\in\mathbb{R}^{L_r\times1536},\quad x_r\in\mathbb{R}^{1536},\quad G_r\in\mathbb{R}^{n_g\times1536},\qquad r\in\{u,v\},\\ &(\widetilde x_r,\widetilde G_r)=\operatorname{Std}_{\mathrm{fit}}(x_r,G_r),\qquad A_r=\operatorname{Enc}(E_r)\in\mathbb{R}^{L_r\times512},\\ &p_{uv}=P\!\left(y_{uv}=1\mid E_u,E_v,x_u,x_v,G_u,G_v\right)\in[0,1]. \end{aligned} \]

Generate, align, and stitch. The shared endpoint generator converts each standardized endpoint and its grounding pool into \(K\) latent neighbor slots. A learned cost aligns the two unordered slot sets with entropic optimal transport; the resulting plan is then used to assemble a typed pair scaffold.

\[ \begin{aligned} S_r&=\operatorname{Gen}(\widetilde x_r,\widetilde G_r) =(H_r,\pi_r,m_r,g_r,Q_r,\widehat A_r),\\ H_r&\in\mathbb{R}^{K\times256},\quad \pi_r,g_r\in[0,1]^K,\quad m_r\in[1,\infty)^K,\quad Q_r\in[0,1]^{K\times n_g},\quad \widehat A_r\in[0,1]^{K\times K},\\ C_{uv}&=\operatorname{Cost}(S_u,S_v),\qquad \Pi_{uv}=\operatorname{Sinkhorn}_{\varepsilon}(C_{uv};\pi_u,\pi_v)\in\mathbb{R}_{+}^{K\times K},\\ \mathcal T_{uv}&=\operatorname{Stitch}(S_u,S_v,\Pi_{uv}),\qquad C_{\mathrm{topo}}^{uv}=\operatorname{STE}(\mathcal T_{uv}),\qquad C_{\mathrm{cont}}^{uv}=\operatorname{Cont}(S_u,S_v). \end{aligned} \]

Condition both directions. The Siamese pair trunk processes \(u\!\to\!v\) and \(v\!\to\!u\) separately. In its final layer, topology first updates the base CLS state; content cross-attention then queries that topology-updated state. Both updates are centered, gated residuals and can be bypassed exactly.

\[ \begin{aligned} c_{rs}^{(0)}&=\operatorname{CLS}\!\left(\operatorname{Trunk}(A_r,A_s)\right),\\ \delta_{rs}^{\mathrm{topo}}&=\operatorname{XAttn}_{\mathrm{topo}}\!\left(c_{rs}^{(0)},C_{\mathrm{topo}}^{rs}\right)-\mu_{\mathrm{topo}},\\ c_{rs}^{\mathrm{topo}}&=c_{rs}^{(0)}+a_{\mathrm{topo}}\tanh(\gamma_{\mathrm{topo}})\,\delta_{rs}^{\mathrm{topo}},\\ \delta_{rs}^{\mathrm{cont}}&=\operatorname{XAttn}_{\mathrm{cont}}\!\left(c_{rs}^{\mathrm{topo}},C_{\mathrm{cont}}^{rs}\right)-\mu_{\mathrm{cont}},\\ c_{rs}^{\mathrm{cond}}&=c_{rs}^{\mathrm{topo}}+a_{\mathrm{cont}}\tanh(\gamma_{\mathrm{cont}})\,\delta_{rs}^{\mathrm{cont}},\\ z_{rs}&=\operatorname{Readout}\!\left(A_r,A_s,c_{rs}^{\mathrm{cond}}\right),\qquad (r,s)\in\{(u,v),(v,u)\}. \end{aligned} \]

Symmetric edge decision. The two directed states are combined by a feature-wise maximum before the binary head, so swapping the queried endpoints leaves the prediction unchanged.

\[ z_{uv}^{\mathrm{sym}}=\operatorname{max}\!\left(z_{uv},z_{vu}\right),\qquad p_{uv}=\sigma\!\left(\operatorname{MLP}(z_{uv}^{\mathrm{sym}})\right)=p_{vu}. \]

Symbols. \(L_r\) is endpoint \(r\)'s token count, \(n_g\) the number of grounding candidates, \(K\) the number of generated slots, and \(y_{uv}\in\{0,1\}\) the queried-edge label. \(\operatorname{Std}_{\mathrm{fit}}\) uses statistics frozen on the fitting universe for both endpoint and grounding features, while \(\operatorname{Enc}\) is the shared raw-token encoder. \(H_r\) are slot states; \(\pi_r\), \(m_r\), and \(g_r\) are slot existence, multiplicity, and grounding gates; \(Q_r\) is the pointer distribution over grounding candidates; and \(\widehat A_r\) is the symmetric within-endpoint slot adjacency. \(\Pi_{uv}\) is the cross-endpoint transport plan, \(\mathcal T_{uv}\) the stitched typed scaffold, and \(C_{\mathrm{topo}}^{rs},C_{\mathrm{cont}}^{rs}\) the topology and content token sequences. The states \(c_{rs}^{(0)}\), \(c_{rs}^{\mathrm{topo}}\), and \(c_{rs}^{\mathrm{cond}}\) are the base, topology-updated, and fully conditioned CLS vectors; \(z_{rs}\) is the directed embedding after \(\operatorname{Readout}\), which combines that CLS vector with mean, max, and attention summaries of both protein-token streams. Finally, \(a_b\in\{0,1\}\) is an exact pathway switch, \(\gamma_b\) a learned scalar gate, \(\mu_b\) the synchronized centering statistic for branch \(b\), and \(\sigma\) the logistic sigmoid.
