artifact, and the formal gate on 2026-07-24. Training was **valid**: the within-checkpoint
liveness (death) rule did not fire on the `full` arm. Given valid training, the gate
returns every applicable failure label rather than a single verdict: one of three
required primary dominance checks passes, one of two guards fails, pathway attribution
establishes no gain, and the structure-destruction control does not pass.

This is a one-seed engineering decision. It licenses no statistical-significance or
cross-seed-robustness claim; E1/E3 remain bound to at least three seeds and

### Liveness (within-checkpoint death rule, `full` arm vs its own `f_logit`)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Residual std | 0.111118 |
| Residual/`f_logit` std ratio | 0.0390165 |
| Spearman(full, f_logit) | 0.9996438 |
| Top-1% overlap vs `f_logit` | 0.9881700 |

Death rule (`residual ratio < 1e-5` AND `Spearman > 0.9999` AND `top-1% overlap >
0.9999`) did **not** fire — training is valid and a scientific verdict is permitted.

### Pathway attribution

Metric: clustering-MMD ratio at the canonical operating point.
`G_full = clu(b0_e2e_f_only) − clu(full) = 11.7718 − 11.9698 = −0.1980648`.
`G_pt = clu(b0_e2e_f_only) − clu(pair_topology) = 11.7718 − 12.4587 = −0.6869907`.
Formal pass requires `G_full > 0` and `G_pt ≥ 0.25 × G_full`. Since `G_full ≤ 0`, the
registered reading fires on its first clause: **no gain is established relative to
matched f-only**; the registered instruction is to withdraw the topology-representation
headline.

### Structure control

Paired bootstrap over the 500 fixed evaluation subgraphs, shared resample indices,
`B = 1000`, seed 0; requires the 2.5th percentile of
`clu(structure_control_6a) − clu(full) > 0`. Measured 2.5th-percentile lower bound is
`0.0`; the check **fails**. Scope is fixed-training-seed evaluator sampling stability
only, not cross-seed inference.

## Failure readings (registered text, verbatim/near-verbatim)

- **Primary fail:** "The stable topology-conditioned encoder does not beat calibrated
  independent scoring at this stage."
- **Guard fail** (matched edge AUPRC): "The model exceeded the registered edge-ranking
  degradation or degree-MMD guard." — specifically the AUPRC guard; the degree-MMD guard
  itself passed.
- **Attribution fail:** "No gain over matched f-only was established, or the topology
  share was insufficient; withdraw the topology-representation headline."
- **Structure-control fail:** "The registered control did not establish that intact
  relational connectivity is necessary beyond retained topology-derived node features"
  at this fixed seed.

Per the registered classification order, `training_invalid` did not apply (liveness
passed), so every applicable label above is reported and the verdict is `cut`.

## Positive and mechanistic findings (within the registered scope)

- **Mechanistic reading:** the conditioning pathway is numerically alive — it moves the
  full logit relative to `f_logit` (residual std `0.111118`, no death-rule trigger) —
  but the signal it carries is degree-like and non-relational rather than
  clustering/community structure: ridge probe R² is `0.381877` for degree versus
  `0.061810` (`0.021433` degree-partialled) for clustering and `0.006019`
  (`0.006170` partialled) for ego-density; Π/shared-neighbor consistency is exactly `0`
  over all `4,096` registered pairs; and the `structure_control_6a` within-pair shuffle
  is inert (identical metrics to `full` to six decimals).
- **From-scratch trunk viability:** with conditioning bypassed, `b0_e2e_f_only`
  approximately reproduces frozen-B0 edge quality (degree-corrected AUPRC `0.728873`
  vs B0's `0.730260`) and exceeds B0's matched BFS-macro GS (`0.326127` vs `0.312151`).
- **Branch dropout is load-bearing:** the `p0` arm (`p_topo = p_cont = 0`) regresses on
  every MMD axis relative to `full` (degree/clustering/spectral `18.3172/14.7475/24.7206`
  vs `13.8037/11.9698/19.2278`), showing the registered branch-dropout regularization
  contributes measurably even though the conditioning pathway itself does not yet carry
  relational signal.

These readings do not establish statistical significance or cross-seed robustness; they
are one-seed engineering-screen observations only.
