# G5 Stage-1 Frozen-S0 Screen: Binding Seed-0 Result

## Verdict and scope

**Binding engineering-screen verdict: `cut`.** The fixed Seed-0 run completed the
registered training, full-candidate fp32 scoring, fidelity/cost diagnostics, and
held-out gate on 2026-07-16. It passed both guards and failed all three required
primary dominance checks. The registered failure reading therefore fires on its
first clause: the frozen-s0 generative imagination apparatus is not load-bearing at
this stage.

This is a one-seed engineering decision. It licenses cutting the frozen-s0 head from
the active G5 build line, but it does **not** establish statistical significance or
cross-seed robustness. E1/E3 remain bound to at least three seeds and Holm-corrected
inference.

## What the repaired run establishes

### 1. The original membership-scale bug is fixed

The normalized membership kernel returns `s1` at an O(1) scale (`2.19 ± 0.058` at
epoch 1), rather than the saturated scale seen in the retained diagnostic. The
candidate artifact is full-resolution fp32. This rules out the old BF16 grid and
pair-invariant `s1` collapse as explanations for the binding result.

### 2. Registered checkpoint selection chose the warm-start model

Epochs 1–6 are reconstruction-only warm start (`L_edge = 0`). Validation AUPRC is
already `0.952522` at epoch 1, the highest value in the run. The best joint-phase
value is `0.952147` at epoch 8 and epoch 30 ends at `0.949478`. The registered
AUPRC-first rule therefore selects epoch 1.

On the full candidate universe, the selected checkpoint remains very close to S0:

- residual/S0 standard-deviation ratio: `7.950 × 10⁻4`;
- Spearman correlation with fresh fp32 S0: `0.999937`;
- top-1% overlap with S0: `0.999853`;
- correlation with the canonical B0 comparator logit: `0.999979`.

The artifact is valid under the pre-registered conjunctive dead-residual guard, but
the selection rule exposes a real blind spot: an edge-loss-ineligible warm-start
checkpoint can win because frozen S0 already saturates the easy validation objective.

### 3. Loss-family competition is measured, not assumed

Across the joint phase, median gradient norms are `0.0375` (edge), `20.64`
(reconstruction), `66.28` (realism), and `0.00367` (SSL). Reconstruction and realism
are therefore about `500×` and `1,682×` the edge norm, respectively. The registered
Kendall fallback does not activate because its largest-family/median ratio stays below
10 (`5.67–7.29`): with two large auxiliary families, the family median masks low-side
edge starvation.

### 4. Imagination fidelity remains weak

Slot recall@k is `0.0` on both train and test. Slot-adjacency/clustering correlation
is weak (Spearman `0.0463` train, `0.1827` test), and the expected-degree deciles
increase from roughly 60 to 83 while realized degree remains roughly 27–32. These
diagnostics support the registered failure reading independently of the selected
checkpoint's near-S0 ranking.

## Locked-decision disposition

The frozen-s0 `(s0,s1,s2)` residual head is retired from the active G5 build line and
retained as:

1. the registered motivating result showing that an anchored scalar extension is
   insufficient under the topology gate; and
2. an ablation rung for the rev-3.0 end-to-end model.

The next G5 build line is the approved rev-3.0 stitched-topology-conditioned pair
encoder: a jointly trained trunk conditioned at representation level by the
structure-only STE and gated cross-attention. The frozen-s0 result satisfies successor
landing condition §14.3(1); it does not itself bind the new architecture, loss,
registration, or success thresholds.
