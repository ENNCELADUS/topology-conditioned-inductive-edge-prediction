The pair-to-topology gap

The final G1 row for a frozen independent pairwise scorer reaches AUROC 0.7055 / AUPRC 0.7303 on degree-corrected negatives. Its assembly has global simple-edge RD 0.9977, yet official PRING BFS-macro GS 0.3122 / RD 0.4223 and MMD ratios 11.9–18.1 times the real-vs-real reference floor. Good edge scores do not imply a plausible network. A later checkpoint-only v3.1 evaluation rerun reached balanced-test AUROC 0.8052 / AUPRC 0.8184 and still produced canonical MMD ratios 11.6–20.0. B0-alt independently produces ratios 13.5–23.5, closing G1's architecture-independence arm.
