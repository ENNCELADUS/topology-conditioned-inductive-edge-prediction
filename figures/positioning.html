ICLR 2027 - Positioning

Topology-Conditioned Inductive Edge Prediction

Edge prediction over unseen nodes splits along two axes: whether the model needs the observed graph at inference, and whether the per-pair decision uses topological structure. Existing work fills three corners. The fourth - inductive and structure-aware - is ours: for each queried pair we build a per-query local scaffold from frozen features, condition the 0/1 decision on it, and grade the assembled graph with realism metrics.

Why the cell is empty - five distinguishing properties
